# Tableau Public Dashboard Design & Notes

## Data Sources (`data/processed/`)
- `monthly_sales.csv`: 25 monthly time points (`month`, `orders`, `revenue`) extracted via `sql/03_monthly_sales.sql`.
- `top_countries.csv`: Country-level revenue, order counts, and unique customer counts extracted via `sql/04_top_countries.sql`.
- `dashboard_customers.csv`: Customer-level merge of K-Means RFM segments (`customer_segments.csv`) and 90-day churn predictions (`churn_predictions.csv`) generated by `src/prepare_dashboard_data.py`.

## Layout Plan (3 Pages)
1. **Page 1 — Executive Overview**: Headline KPIs (Total Revenue, Active Customers, Total Orders), Monthly Revenue Trend line chart, and Top 10 Countries by Revenue bar chart.
2. **Page 2 — Customer Segments**: Customer count per segment, revenue share per segment, average RFM summary table, and tailored marketing recommendations per segment.
3. **Page 3 — Churn Risk & Retention Action List**: Risk-level distribution (High / Medium / Low), interactive Segment and Country filters, and a prioritized High-Risk Customer table sorted by churn probability.

## Design Standards
- Consistent segment color palette across all 3 pages.
- Insight-driven chart titles stating the key takeaway rather than generic variable labels.
